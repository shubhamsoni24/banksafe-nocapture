Why BankSafe NoCapture?

🏦

200+ Banks Covered

Detects all scheduled Indian banks (RBI list), major US/UK/EU banks, card portals, and the new .bank.in domains — out of the box.

🔕

Not Annoying

One alert per site, per tab. It won't nag you while you scroll or tab away and back. The notification stays until you dismiss it.

⚡

Ridiculously Light

Under 2 KB of JavaScript. No frameworks, no bundlers, no build step. It installs in seconds and uses zero memory when idle.

🔒

100% Private

No network calls. No tracking. No analytics. No storage of browsing data. Everything stays on your machine, always.

⚙️

Fully Customizable

Add your own bank domains through the settings page. Import/export keywords as JSON. Toggle protection on or off instantly.

🟢

Visual Status Badge

Green toolbar badge when you're on a banking site, grey when disabled. One glance tells you everything.

Install in 60 seconds

1

Download

Grab the ZIP from GitHub and extract it anywhere on your computer.

2

Open Extensions

Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer Mode.

3

Load Unpacked

Click "Load unpacked" and pick the extracted folder. That's it.
